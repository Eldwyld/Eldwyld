From the Eldwyld Archives

The Witch's Almanac is the second volume of the Eldwyld Archives — a realm kept at the crossroads of imagination and implementation, where the mystical and the digital converge. It is undated on purpose: a planner that molts with you, year after year, printed as the wheel turns.

Your license

Licensed for personal use: print as many copies as you wish, for yourself and for gifts, forever. Please don't resell, redistribute, or upload the files themselves — send fellow travelers to the Archives instead.

Sibling volumes

Volume I, The Forager's Grimoire, keeps the green side of the practice — plant profiles, field pages, hearth recipes and workings. The two are bound to share a binder.

Print notes

US Letter and A4 editions included · single-sided · near-black ink on any paper · a three-ring binder makes the almanac perennial.
