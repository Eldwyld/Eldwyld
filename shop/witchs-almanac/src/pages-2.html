the first station · Oct 31 – Nov 1

Samhain

The veil thins; the beloved dead are guests;
the year turns its face to the dark.

Colors

black, deep orange, bone white

Herbs & boughs

rosemary, mugwort, apple leaf

On the table

apples, pomegranate, dark bread

This year it falls on a
Under amoon
How I will mark the day
Altar, hearth & table — and who is remembered
Afterward — what the season said

the second station · Dec 20 – 23 · winter solstice

Yule

The longest night, kept bright —
the sun is born small and stubborn.

Colors

evergreen, gold, ember red

Herbs & boughs

pine, holly, cinnamon

On the table

spiced cider, roasted roots, oranges

This year it falls on a
Under amoon
How I will mark the longest night
Light kept burning — candles, fires, kindnesses
Afterward — what returned with the light

the third station · Feb 1 – 2

Imbolc

Under the snow, the first stirring;
every candle lit is a promise kept.

Colors

white, pale gold, first green

Herbs & boughs

bay, rowan, the earliest shoots

On the table

milk, butter, seeded bread

This year it falls on a
Under amoon
How I will mark the quickening
What is seeded now — projects, plots, beginnings
Afterward — first signs noticed

the fourth station · Mar 19 – 22 · spring equinox

Ostara

Light and dark stand level,
and everything green leans forward.

Colors

spring green, violet, shell pink

Herbs & boughs

violet, nettle, birch

On the table

eggs, honey cakes, young greens

This year it falls on a
Under amoon
How I will mark the balance
What is planted — in soil and in self
Afterward — what broke ground
