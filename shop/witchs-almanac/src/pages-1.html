How to Keep This Almanac

No printed dates, no wasted Januaries. This almanac begins
whenever you do, and lasts as long as you keep it.

Print by the year's need. Twelve month grids, a weekly page for every week you plan on paper, daily pages for the thick of things. Reprint forever — the almanac never expires.

Open with The Year's Turning. Once a year (at Samhain or in January — keeper's choice), fill in the new and full moons and mark the feasts you mean to keep.

The eight station pages carry each sabbat's dates, colors, herbs and table, with room to plan the day and record what it taught. They are the almanac's heart.

Two weekly layouts — cards for those who plan in blocks, bands for list-keepers. Use one, or alternate with the seasons.

Daily Tendings tracks your small practices moon-month by moon-month — one circle a day, shaded as kept.

Bind and re-bind. A three-ring binder lets the almanac molt each year: archive the filled pages, print fresh ones, keep the sabbat wisdom pages where they are.

Printing — US Letter or A4, single-sided, ink-friendly near-black.
