# The SpiralSightedVisions Brand Bible
*Witchpunk Sticker-Zine Gnosis — canonical edition*

The shop is **SpiralSightedVisions**. (Eldwyld is the novel; it does not appear
in commercial products.) This document is the law for every asset in the store.

## 1 · Core Visual DNA (the non-negotiables)
- **The die-cut boundary** — every standalone subject wrapped in a bold uniform
  white outline (≈1.5–2% of canvas width; see Amendment B), separating it fully
  from the background. Reads at thumbnail size; mimics the peel of premium vinyl.
- **Organic wobbly linework** — mechanical perfection is forbidden. Thick
  hand-drawn outer silhouettes, finer tapered interior lines. Sharpie assertion,
  crayon drag.
- **Halftone & grain shadows** — never smooth gradients. Depth comes from 90s
  screen-print halftone dots, cross-hatch, or heavy grain, in a darker shifted
  hue of the base pastel — never pure black.
- **Melting & dripping motifs** — gravity is soft; reality yields. At least one
  zone of translucent melt or drip per asset (wax, ink, pastel borders).
- **Gooey high-shine highlights** — bulbous white speculars like liquid surface
  tension, always with four-pointed diamond sparkles ✦ at peak focal points.
- **Heavy-lidded expression** — any faced entity gets a drooping upper lid and
  thick lash line: sleepy, knowing, indifferent.

## 2 · The Witchpunk Color Grimoire (70 / 20 / 10)
70% pastel base · 20% mid-tone shadow · 10% high-contrast accent.
- **Base pastels**: Ghost Lavender (faded dream-purple substrate) · Parchment /
  Paper Bone (aged warm off-whites) · Dream Fill (milky MS-Paint tones)
- **Accents**: Feral Primary (unhinged cherry red — emotional punctuation) ·
  Sacred Night (deep indigo — the unseen void, definitive boundaries) ·
  Glitch-Prophet (cyan/neon green — digital corruption, unexpected life) ·
  Sticker Gold (dollar-store foil yellow — sacred illumination)

## 3 · Texture & Rendering (the zine-craft)
Cracked dried glue, construction-paper tooth, slick vinyl finish. Low-res
artifacts, ASCII corruption, raw pixel edges — as garnish (see Amendment C).
Scattered marginalia, multiple handstyles, graffiti tags in the margins.

## 4 · Shape Language
Bottom-heavy everything — books, creatures, architecture widen at the base.
No lethal points: athames and thorns get thick rounded edges, safe to touch.
Occult symmetry (sigils, mandalas) rendered wobbly and slightly asymmetric.

## 5 · Floating Debris & Thematic Lore
Every primary subject orbited by 3–5 lore-bearing floaters: broken crayon nubs,
googly eyes, ✦ sparkles, pastel drips, **tiny frogs** (prophetic ordinary-sacred
witnesses), torn receipts and bus tickets with margin notes, runes, glitch
fragments, ASCII.

## 6 · Application — Shopify digital products
- **Thumbnails**: treat like a sticker pack — the guide/book rendered chunky and
  rounded, thick white die-cut border, topic-relevant debris orbiting.
- **PDF layouts**: decorative frames of melting pastel lines or repeating tiny
  skulls/hearts/four-point stars (print register applies — Amendment A).
- **Type**: workhorse sans for readability (the "neutral observer"); bold
  hand-lettering or unironic Comic Sans (Comic Neue in our pipeline) for
  headings, callouts, annotations.
- **UI & icons**: level-1 stickerification — core pastel shape, thick hand-drawn
  outline, one gooey highlight, white die-cut border.

## Amendments (adopted with the bible)

**A · The print register.** The bible is a screen aesthetic; printables must not
be ink-hungry. For PDF interiors: near-black wobbly linework + halftone on
Paper Bone ground, pastels reserved for covers and optional "full-color" pages.
Full pastel gnosis applies to stickers, thumbnails, social, and store chrome.

**B · Relative units.** All pixel specs scale as % of canvas width: die-cut
border ≈ 1.5–2%, outer strokes ≈ 0.5–0.8%, interior lines ≈ 0.15–0.3%.

**C · Glitch cap on storefront assets.** Compression artifacts and pixel-crunch
are garnish around the subject, never on it — platforms recompress images and
double-degradation reads as low quality to cold buyers.

**D · Legibility floor.** Load-bearing words (product names, prices, "instant
download") always in the neutral-observer sans at ≥4.5:1 contrast against their
ground. Expressive type carries mood, never information.

## The two registers in practice
- **Witchpunk register** (this bible): store identity, thumbnails, social pins,
  sticker products, zine-style guides.
- **Archive register** (Vols. I–II interiors): antique engraved line art, Cinzel/
  Cormorant/IM Fell, cream-and-bronze. It stays — it is why the printables look
  premium — but its products get witchpunk *thumbnails* per §6.
