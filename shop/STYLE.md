# The Eldwyld Style Guide

One brand, two registers. Everything the Archives ships should be recognizable
at a glance as Eldwyld — but not everything should whisper.

## The two registers

### 1 · The Archive register (established — Vols. I & II)
The voice of the books themselves. Antique, engraved, apothecary-label.
- **Line**: fine strokes (0.6–2.5pt), stroke-based botanical line art, ornamental
  frames with corner flourishes
- **Type**: Cinzel Decorative (display) · Cinzel (labels, small caps) ·
  Cormorant Garamond (body) · IM Fell English italic (flavor text)
- **Ground**: cream paper `#fdfcf8`, ink `#2b2620`, bronze `#7a6a4f`,
  faint `#a99a83`, moss `#4f5a44`
- **Where it lives**: product interiors, covers, anything printed

### 2 · The Wyld register (new — the "edgy sticker" voice)
Same soul, louder mouth. Chunky, high-contrast, flash-sheet energy —
the Archive's marginalia come alive.
- **Line**: bold uniform strokes (6–10pt at 100px scale), closed shapes,
  sticker-white keylines, solid fills allowed
- **Motifs**: the same familiars — moons, mushrooms, sigils, ferns, jars,
  candles, acorns — plus edgier cousins: skulls with sprouting flora, moths,
  snakes in wreaths, hands holding light, "trust the wyld" banners
- **Palette**: ink-black grounds, cream + bronze from the Archive, with one
  permitted flare color per set (candle-flame amber, poison-bottle green,
  hex-purple)
- **Type**: Cinzel for banner text; hand-lettered-look allowed
- **Where it lives**: sticker sheets, social graphics, pin overlays, apparel
  later, TikTok/IG covers

**The rule that keeps it one brand**: shared motifs, shared bronze-and-ink DNA,
shared ✦. The registers differ in line weight and volume, never in mythology.

## Product: printable sticker sheets (Wyld register's debut)
**"Wyld Marks — Sigils & Specimens"** · $5–6
- 3–4 sheets of print-and-cut stickers (moons, mushroom clusters, sigil badges,
  banners, tiny familiars) as PDF + individual PNGs with transparent backgrounds
  (Cricut/Silhouette-friendly)
- Made with the same SVG pipeline — bold-stroke symbol set rendered at high res
- Natural bundle: **Almanac + Wyld Marks** ($15) — planner people are sticker people;
  this is the highest-converting add-on in the planner category
- Later, zero-work physical upsell: the same art on die-cut stickers via
  print-on-demand (Printful/Gelato) — but printable-first, physical only after
  the printable proves demand

## Naming pattern
- Volumes (Archive register): *The ___'s ___* — Forager's Grimoire,
  Witch's Almanac, Worldbuilder's Codex
- Sets (Wyld register): *Wyld ___* — Wyld Marks, Wyld Banners, Wyld Familiars
