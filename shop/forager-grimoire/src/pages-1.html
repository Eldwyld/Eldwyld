How to Keep This Grimoire

This is a working book — print it, bind it, spill tea on it.
It is meant to grow moss and marginalia.

Print what you need, as often as you need. Every page is undated and reusable. Print a fresh plant profile for each new ally you meet; print twelve moon trackers for a year of moons.

The Materia pages (plant profiles & field identification) are the heart of the book — one spread per plant, gathered over seasons into your own local materia.

The Field pages (trip log, seasonal calendar, ethics) travel with you. A clipboard and a pencil in the woods beat a perfect binder at home.

The Hearth pages (teas, salves, simmer pots) record what you make from what you gather — with dates, ratios and results, so your craft compounds.

The Workings pages (spell log, correspondences, sigils, dreams) keep the subtle side of the practice. Note moon and season; patterns emerge in the margins.

Bind it your way — three-ring binder, discbound, or a simple folder. The contents page at the front is a fill-in index: number your pages as your book grows.

A note on printing — pages are designed for US Letter or A4, single-sided,
in ink-friendly near-black. Cardstock makes fine covers; 24 lb paper takes fountain pen well.
