From the Eldwyld Archives

The Forager's Grimoire was made in the Eldwyld — a realm kept at the crossroads of imagination and implementation, where the mystical and the digital converge. It was designed to be printed, bound, carried into wet grass, and filled with a life's worth of green knowledge.

Your license

This grimoire is licensed for personal use: print as many copies as you wish, for yourself and for gifts, forever. Please don't resell, redistribute, or upload the files themselves — send fellow travelers to the Archives instead.

Nothing in these pages is medical advice, and no journal replaces a qualified guide, an up-to-date regional field manual, or common sense. Foraging carries real risk: misidentification can be fatal. You are the only one responsible for what you harvest and what you do with it.

Print notes

US Letter and A4 editions included · single-sided · near-black ink on any paper · pairs well with a three-ring binder, pressed leaves, and a pot of something warm.
