# Marketing Kit — The Forager's Grimoire

The niche: "grimoire printable / book of shadows pages / green witch journal."
Discovery happens on **Pinterest search** (evergreen, compounding — a good pin
keeps sending traffic for years) with Instagram/TikTok as amplifiers.

## Pinterest setup (once, ~15 min)
1. Business account for **SpiralSightedVisions** (free) → claim the shop domain once one exists.
2. Boards: `Grimoire Pages & Book of Shadows` · `Green Witch Printables` ·
   `Foraging & Wildcrafting` · `Cottagecore Stationery` · `Wheel of the Year & Moon Magic`.
3. Pin the ready-made images (in `forager-grimoire/dist/`):
   - `marketing/pin.png` (the designed 2:3 pin)
   - `marketing/hero-square.png`
   - `marketing/whats-inside.png`
   - single pages as their own pins: `pages/page-01.png`, `page-05.png`,
     `page-06.png`, `page-07.png`, `page-11.png`, `page-13.png`, `page-16.png`
   Each pin links to the Shopify product URL.
4. Cadence that compounds: 2–3 pins/week (re-pin the same product with a
   different image/title — Pinterest treats each as fresh content). 10 minutes,
   once a week, batched.
